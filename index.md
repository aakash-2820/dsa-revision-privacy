# DSA Revision Buddy — Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** October 19, 2026

DSA Revision Buddy is a personal educational assistant designed to help users revise Data Structures and Algorithms through WhatsApp.

## Information We Process

Depending on the features used, the service may process:

- WhatsApp phone numbers and messages sent to the bot.
- DSA learning notes and doubts explicitly provided by users.
- Quiz answers, scores, and revision progress.
- Messaging preferences and notification status.

## How Information Is Used

This information is used to deliver revision notes, generate quizzes, assess answers, provide learning explanations, and send requested reminders.

## Third-Party Services

The service uses WhatsApp Business Platform (Meta) for messaging and n8n Cloud for automation. When AI features are enabled, learning material and quiz responses may also be processed by the selected AI service provider.

## Data Retention and Security

Learning records and quiz progress may be retained to provide ongoing revision. Access to these records is restricted through the service's configured security controls.

The retention period and deletion procedures will be documented and updated before the service is offered to other users.

## User Choices and Data Deletion

Users may request to stop receiving messages or request deletion of their stored information by contacting the developer.
